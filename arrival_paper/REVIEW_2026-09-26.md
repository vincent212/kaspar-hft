# Review of paper_v2.tex, 2026-09-26

Full read by four parallel reviewers (front matter to Part I; Sections 3–4; Sections 5–8;
Part III to appendices), plus mechanical checks of labels, references and citations. The
most serious findings were checked against the file before being listed. Line numbers are
as of commit `e34637e`.

## Errors

1. **"Closer than the publisher period, they reach the receiver in one packet."**
   Lines 2655 (§4.9) and 5101 (Conclusion, "events below the publisher period").
   Contradicts Table 12: when the engine processes two transactions within a microsecond,
   the publisher sends them in separate packets 84 % of the time. Fix: they arrive one
   publisher period apart, or less often in one packet.
2. **Conjecture box, §5.8, line 3283:** "delivers that information 0.4 to 15 ms late"
   mixes a median (about 0.4 ms at moderate bursts) with a p99 (15 ms at the heaviest).
3. **Contributions, lines 412–414:** the null streams show the tail is "not their size,
   their packetisation, the packet rate" with no service-time scope. Contradicts the
   long-packet finding at the production operating point. Add "at service times above the
   publisher period".
4. **§4.1, lines 1586 and 1659:** "at T ≤ 4 µs every stream sits at the no-queue latency".
   True for p99, not p99.9: the Poisson p99.9 at T = 4 is 5.78 (line 1502), as
   Corollary 1 predicts. Scope to p99.
