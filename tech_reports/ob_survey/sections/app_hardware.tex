\section{Commercial trading hardware}
\label{sec:app-vendors}
% ================================================================

This appendix supports Section~\ref{sec:hw-classes}, which compares the classes of hardware; vendor figures change
quickly and are collected here rather than in the body.

Most production hardware for trading is commercial. Table~\ref{tab:vendors} lists representative offerings with
the figures their makers publish; STAC-audited results are in Section~\ref{sec:hw-audited}. The STAC-T0 result of an
Exegy framework on an AMD card is shown here for reference~\citep{exegy_stac2024}. The figures describe different
stages (a transceiver loopback, a trigger, a book build, a model inference) and are best read each on its own terms;
the AMD figure is a transceiver latency, as AMD specifies it.
