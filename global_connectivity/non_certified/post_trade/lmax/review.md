# LMAX post-trade client: review list

Built from section 10 of `feed.md`. TR is LMAX's Broker Trading `fix-spec.json`
(release 370, 2023-12-02); DC the MTF Drop Copy `fix-spec.json` (2.7). Nothing was
checked against LMAX: there was no demo key file.

## Needs your decision

### 1. One identifier for a fill across AE and ExecutionReport

**Question.** The same fill arrives as an ExecutionReport (F) on the order
session and as a TradeCaptureReport (AE). TR defines ExecID as the id of an
execution message, "unique over all time", and SecondaryExecID as "the execution
ID as generated by the MTF", set only on trades (TR 17, 527). It does not say
whether an AE carries the same ExecID as the F. DC, for the MTF drop copy,
calls ExecID "the unique identifier for each LMAX trade" and recommends ExecID
plus Side as the key.

**Options.**

- (a) `OrderID:SecondaryExecID` (ExecID when SecondaryExecID is absent or "0").
  (Implemented.)
- (b) `OrderID:ExecID`.
- (c) `ExecID:Side`, as DC recommends for drop copy.

**Recommendation.** (a), then check it in the live run: place one demo fill and
compare the F's and the AE's 17 and 527. If AE and F share ExecID, (a) and (b)
both work; if SecondaryExecID differs between them, (a) double counts and must
change. Until checked, position keeping should not take both inputs at once.

**What depends on it.** Whether a fill seen on both inputs is counted once (test
`ReportsReachTheSubscriberOnce` holds only under the reading).

### 2. Linking corrections and cancels

**Question.** ExecType G (TradeCorrect) and H (TradeCancel) are listed in TR 150,
but TR's dictionary has no ExecRefID, so the report does not name the trade it
changes.

**Options.**

- (a) Assume G and H carry the original trade's SecondaryExecID; without one,
  log the report and do not apply it. (Implemented.)
- (b) Treat every G or H as unlinkable: log and alert, change nothing.

**Recommendation.** (a), with the alert of (b) in production: the OM already logs
each G and H as an error for a person to check. LMAX should be asked how a
broken trade is reported.

**What depends on it.** Whether a busted trade leaves the position.

### 3. A second session for trade capture

**Question.** TR does not say whether a user may hold two Broker Trading sessions
at once (one for orders, one for TradeCaptureReportRequest).

**Options.** (a) The client takes the OM's `ExecCopy` messages and, when given a
session factory, also runs its own session with a TradeCaptureReportRequest.
(Implemented, both optional.) (b) No own session: the OM's copies only, plus an
AD sent on the OM's session after each logon.

**Recommendation.** (a) with its own session only if LMAX gives separate CompIDs
for it; otherwise run it from the OM's copies, and request the day's trades
through the OM's session after a long disconnect.

### 4. Trading day

**Question.** The request's start time and the ledger's daily clear need the
start of LMAX's trading day. TR gives the session schedule (17:02 New York) and
TradeDate as "local time at place of trade".

**Reading taken.** 17:00 America/New_York, with US daylight saving
(`trading_day_start_ns`, tested across both DST changes of 2026).

**Recommendation.** Keep; a request from 17:00 includes everything a 17:02 day
holds.

### 5. Quantity unit in TradeCapture

**Question.** TradeCapture.qty is an integer; LMAX quantities are contracts in
steps of 0.1.

**Reading taken.** Kaspar units (LastQty / 0.1), as the order manager and the SOM
count them; a quantity that is not a whole unit is refused and logged.

**Recommendation.** Keep for position keeping inside Kaspar. An STP publisher
that needs base-currency amounts multiplies by 1,000 (0.1 contract of 10,000).

## Settled

| Item | How it was settled |
|---|---|
| No TradeCaptureReportAck | Not in TR's Broker Trading dictionary (the MTF drop copy has one, DC AR) |
| TradeRequestID length | TR 568: at most 16 characters; the client sends `T` and a counter |
| Price precision | LMAX's LastPx text is kept (mantissa and decimals), not a double |
| Replay at every logon | the ledger drops what it holds (`ReconnectReplayAddsNothing`) |

## Low impact

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Whether SubscriptionRequestType 1 streams new trades to a session that did not place them (`feed.md` section 10, item 6) | Not relied on: trades placed through the OM reach the ledger from the OM's own report copies. Matters only for trades placed elsewhere on the same account, which otherwise arrive only in the TradeCaptureReportRequest sent at the next logon. |
| trade_time_ns from UTC midnight | LMAX's timestamps are UTC; TradeCapture asks for the venue's local midnight, which LMAX does not define |
| ExecutionReport has no TradeDate | the UTC date of TransactTime is used for reports from the order session |
| Commission fields | parsed (TR 12, 13, 479), not carried by TradeCapture |
